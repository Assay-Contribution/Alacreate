Contribute's
Manifesto

Every human has a soul and a purpose: to create. Contributors believe that creation that adds to the dignity and care of the earth and all its passengers is the highest expression of a human soul. Contribute is a tool to capture contributions small and great in an effort to amplify, celebrate, credit, and collaborate, together.
